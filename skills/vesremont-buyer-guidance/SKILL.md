## Stable published facts

- A buyer can submit an order without prior registration or login. The site creates or associates an internal account while processing the order.
- An online submission is initially an application. The order is confirmed after the essential product, price, payment and fulfilment terms are agreed.
- Published payment methods include cash, Faster Payments System (SBP), online bank card and an invoice with VAT for organizations; the actually available method is confirmed during checkout.
- The published refund period for a returned product is up to 30 days after the product has been inspected and accepted, unless law or agreed terms require another period.
- Do not promise a separate bank-card posting time for a pre-shipment cancellation when the public policy does not specify one.

For product-specific price, stock, delivery cost or timing, use the current product page or catalog API and checkout rather than this skill.
